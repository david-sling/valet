title: After a session
description: What happens when a session ends, what Valet keeps on your Mac, and how to export a record.
---

## When a session ends

A session ends when you end it, when the vendor ends it, or when it reaches its time limit. At that moment Valet:

1. Stops accepting requests.
2. Stops every program the session started.
3. Drops every permission you gave.
4. Seals and signs the session's log.

Nothing carries over to another session. A new session starts from nothing, with a new code.

If Valet quits while a session is open, it ends the session first. If your Mac loses power or Valet crashes, the session can't be resumed; it ends at its time limit, and the record shows "Valet closed before it ended".

## Past sessions

Valet keeps a record of each session in the **Past** list: who asked, why, when, how it ended, the permissions, and every request with what happened to it.

It never keeps the output of commands or the contents of files. The record stays on your Mac and is not synced or uploaded. Declined sessions aren't kept.

## Exporting a record

Open a past session and choose **Export** to save it as JSON, or to print it or save it as a PDF. You can share it with your IT team or keep it for your own records.

## The signed log

When a session ends, Valet signs its log with a key kept in your Mac's keychain and uploads it, so the vendor can see the same record you can. The relay keeps it for 24 hours, then deletes it.
